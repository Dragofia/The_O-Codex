Codex Guide

Codex Guide & Sharing Tips

Quick tips for navigating, searching, and sharing lore entries with your campaign party.

🗺️

Interactive World Map Mode

Switch seamlessly between the O-Codex and the World Map using the hover mode bar at the very top of your screen! Explore territories, place pins with lore notes, and jump directly from settlements to their map coordinates.

🔗

Direct Link Sharing

Every single entry and category has its own direct link! When you browse or open an entry, your browser address bar updates automatically. Copy the URL from your address bar or click the Copy Link button inside any entry modal to send it to players (e.g. in Discord)—they'll open straight to that entry.

🔍

Smart Typo & Phonetic Search

Don't remember the exact spelling? The search bar tolerates typos and uses sound-alike phonetic matching for fantasy names. It also checks aliases, keywords, and tags to surface the right cards fast.

📜

Category Views & Quests Filter

Use the category tabs above the search bar to zoom in on specific topics (Settlements, Eschatons, Lore, etc.). In the Quests view, an extra status filter appears so you can toggle between Ongoing and Completed missions.

⚔️

Combat Quick Stats & Upgrades

Manifests and Divions feature quick-reference action, range, and charge stats for fast lookup during combat. Linked upgrade paths also provide clickable cross-references connecting items with their associated Spire quests.

⚔️

Character Sheets & Party Dice Tray

Switch to the Characters tab (C) to view party vitals, equipment, magic items, DM boons, and canon Divions. Click any stat, attack, or the floating dice button (D) to roll polyhedral dice shared in real time across the party!

⌨️

Global Keyboard Shortcuts & Quick-Bar

Navigate and search effortlessly from any view or device using these built-in hotkeys:

Ctrl + K / / Instant search & quick jump from anywhere (Codex or Map)
M Toggle between O-Codex and World Map
C Toggle Party Character Sheets
D / Ctrl + R Open Party Dice Roller Tray & Live Feed
1 – 5 Quick-open recent/pinned entries in the bottom Quick-Bar
Esc Close open modals, cancel tools, or dismiss map drawers
? Toggle this Guide & Shortcuts Reference
P Find Party (Center map camera directly on the party token)
Ctrl + Shift + D Toggle Dungeon Master Mode & map tools
